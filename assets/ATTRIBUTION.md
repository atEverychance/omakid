# Bundled asset attribution

## Flags

- Flag of Canada SVG: public-domain national flag artwork, retrieved from Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Canada.svg
- Flag of Québec SVG: public-domain flag artwork, retrieved from Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Quebec.svg

The files are stored as `flags/en.svg` and `flags/fr.svg` because they are language controls in Omakid, not claims that one flag uniquely represents a language.

## Initial spoken labels

The English WAV files were generated locally with macOS Samantha. The French WAV files were generated locally with macOS Amélie (`fr_CA`). They are initial production labels and may be replaced one-for-one by family recordings without changing the shell contract.

## Existing artwork

Omakid tile and animal artwork was created for this project. Application and offline-content licensing remains with each upstream project; prepared offline content carries its own `ATTRIBUTION.md`.
