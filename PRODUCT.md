# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Omakid is for two pre-reader girls aged six and seven using refurbished Dell Inspiron 15-3531 laptops with 1366×768 displays, trackpads, 4 GB RAM, and mechanical hard drives. They work in English and Canadian French and must be able to choose, launch, and leave activities without reading or adult help.

## Product Purpose

Omakid turns current Omarchy Quattro plumbing into a calm, offline-first child desktop. Success means a child can identify herself, switch language, open Painting, Letters, Games, or Stories, and always return home with one physical key. The machine remains useful without internet access and supportable as a small deterministic overlay rather than a permanent upstream fork.

## Brand Personality

Playful, calm, empowering. The interface should feel like a treasured personal object: direct enough to understand immediately, lively enough to invite exploration, and quiet enough not to compete with the activity.

## Anti-references

- Adult Omarchy surfaces: command palettes, terminal-first navigation, dense menus, status bars, and keyboard chords.
- Text-dependent navigation, setup forms, confirmation dialogs, or password gates in the child's normal path.
- Generic educational dashboards, reward-loop clutter, mascot chatter, gradients, glassmorphism, and identical card grids.
- Decorative animation or novelty that delays an action.

## Design Principles

1. **Recognition before reading.** Every core choice is a large picture with a pre-recorded spoken label.
2. **One place, one action.** Home presents exactly four activities; launched apps own the screen until Home is pressed.
3. **Ownership without setup debt.** Colour and animal choices are permanent, immediate, reversible affordances rather than a blocking wizard.
4. **Offline is the baseline.** Core apps, voices, stories, and artwork must work with Wi-Fi disabled.
5. **Speed bumps, not surveillance.** The parental layer shapes defaults without pretending to be a security boundary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core navigation never depends on text, colour alone, hover precision, or internet access. Targets are sized for developing motor control and a trackpad. Every choice has visible focus/hover feedback and spoken English and Canadian French labels. Motion is brief, state-driven, and disabled under reduced-motion preferences. Foreground/background pairings maintain readable contrast; language switching does not require logout or a password.
